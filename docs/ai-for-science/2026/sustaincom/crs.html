Final Paper Submission – SustainCom 2026

Final paper submission link: coming soon

Submission Checklist

Please prepare and submit the following three items:

  1. Camera-Ready Manuscript (PDF)
  2. LaTeX Source Files
  3. Completed and Signed Copyright Form (PDF)

Please note that the absence of any of these three required components will result in the exclusion of the paper from the Conference Proceedings.

Detailed Instructions

1. Camera-Ready Manuscript (PDF)

Format: The camera-ready manuscript must follow the IEEE Computer Society Proceedings Format. Detailed formatting guidelines and templates are available at: https://template-selector.ieee.org/

LaTeX Users: Please use the IEEEtran class and style files to generate your PDF.

Page Limit:

  • Full Regular Paper: Up to 8 complimentary pages, with up to 2 additional pages available for purchase (maximum 10 pages).
  • Regular Paper or Special Session Paper: Up to 6 complimentary pages, with up to 2 additional pages available for purchase (maximum 8 pages).

Over-length charges: RMB 340 (USD$50)

2. LaTeX Source Files

You must submit a ZIP/RAR archive containing all necessary files to compile your paper correctly. This includes:

  • The main .tex file
  • The IEEEtran .cls and .sty files
  • All bibliography files
  • All image files (.eps, .pdf, .png, .jpg)

Crucially, your source files must compile without errors to produce the exact same PDF you submitted.

Please note that only LaTeX submissions are accepted. Word files will not be processed.

3. Completed and Signed Copyright Form (PDF)

Important Note on Corresponding Author:

Each paper must designate exactly one corresponding author. This individual is solely responsible for completing and signing the copyright form on behalf of all co-authors.

Instructions:

  1. Download the copyright form from the conference website: coming soon
  2. Fill in all required fields electronically (Paper Title, Author List, Conference Name, etc.).
  3. The corresponding author must sign the form using a digital signature or by printing, hand-signing, and scanning the form.
  4. Save the completed form as a PDF.

We urge authors to submit their final camera-ready papers before November 30, 2026 (AoE) to ensure a smooth and efficient publication process.

Please note that any changes to the list of authors or the paper title during the preparation of your camera-ready paper must be communicated with valid reasons by emailing ispa-2026@googlegroups.com and must be approved by the conference chairs before November 30, 2026 (AoE). Failure to comply may result in your paper being delayed or excluded from the proceedings.

For any questions, please contact ispa-2026@googlegroups.com and include the full author names and paper ID (the 10-digit ID assigned by EDAS).

Some papers originally submitted as Full Regular Papers may, based on review results, be accepted as Regular Papers. In such cases, authors will need to reduce the number of pages accordingly when preparing the camera-ready version. At least one author of each accepted paper is required to register and present the work at the conference.
