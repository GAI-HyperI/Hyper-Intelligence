Zhengzhou

About Zhengzhou

Zhengzhou, the capital of Henan Province, is nestled in the Central Plains, the cradle of Chinese civilization. As an ancient capital with over 3,000 years of history dating back to the Shang Dynasty, it now serves as a core driver of the modern Central Plains urban cluster. The city is home to profound cultural heritage, including the Shang Dynasty ruins and the legendary Shaolin Temple, while also showcasing contemporary vitality through the modern skyline of Zhengdong New Area, its nationwide “米-shaped” high-speed rail network, and thriving logistics industry. Visitors can stroll along Ruyi Lake, where the city harmoniously blends with waterscapes, savor local specialties like braised noodles and spicy soup, and experience the blend of history and modernity that defines the inclusive and dynamic spirit of Central Plains culture.

Transportation in Zhengzhou

Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport

Zhengzhou Xinzheng International Airport is a major aviation hub in central China, offering numerous direct flights to and from major cities across Asia, Europe, and other parts of the world, providing convenience for both international and domestic travelers. The airport is located approximately 37 kilometers (about 23 miles) from downtown Zhengzhou. Travelers can reach the city center via various transportation options, including taxis, ride-hailing services (such as Didi), airport shuttles, and public transit. Zhengzhou's metro system provides efficient public transportation within the city, and the nearest metro station to the city center is within easy walking distance, allowing convenient access to key areas.

Your Neighbourhood

Located just a short walk from the Millennium Convention Center, the Ruyi Lake area offers a perfect urban escape for conference attendees. A stroll along the elegant imperial lake boardwalk will lead you past the shimmering Ruyi Lake and through the lush Red-White Flower Park, where you can enjoy refreshing greenery and stunning views of Zhengdong New District's skyline. This tranquil route provides an ideal way to unwind between sessions while appreciating the harmony of modern architecture and natural beauty. Within minutes, you'll find yourself at the Dennis Seven Heaven Shopping Center - particularly its 2nd, 6th, and 7th sections - offering diverse dining options from local cuisine to international flavors, along with plentiful shopping opportunities. This convenient blend of natural relaxation and urban convenience makes it perfect for conference participants looking to maximize their limited free time.
