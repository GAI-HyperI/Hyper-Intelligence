Submission Checklist

Please prepare and submit the following three items:
  • Camera-Ready Manuscript (PDF)
  • Source Files (LaTeX or Word)
  • Completed and Signed Copyright Form (PDF)
Please note the absence of any of these three required components will result in the exclusion of the papers from the Conference Proceedings.

Detailed Instructions

  1. Camera-Ready Manuscript (PDF)

    • Format: Camera-ready manuscript must follow the Springer LNCS Format. Detailed formatting guidelines and templates are available at:
      https://www.springer.com/gp/computer-science/lncs/conference-proceedings-guidelines.
      • LaTeX Users: Please use the supplied class and style files (.cls, .sty) to generate your PDF.
      • MS Word Users: Adhere precisely to the template's formatting instructions. Do not alter margins, font styles, or sizes.
    • Page Limit:
      • Regular Paper: 20 pages, with up to 4 additional pages available for purchase at RMB 150 (USD $20) per extra page.
      • Research/Workshop Paper: 12 pages, with up to 4 additional pages available for purchase at RMB 150 (USD $20) per extra page.

  2. Source Files

    • LaTeX Users: You must submit a ZIP/RAR archive containing ALL necessary files to compile your paper correctly. This includes:
      • The main .tex file
      • The supplied .cls, .sty, and .bib files
      • All image files (.eps, .pdf, .png, .jpg)
      • Crucially, your source files must compile without errors to produce the exact same PDF you submitted.
    • MS Word Users: Submit your .docx or .doc file with all images and equations embedded.

  3. Completed and Signed Copyright Form (PDF)

    • Important Note on Corresponding Author:
      • Each paper must designate exactly one corresponding author. This individual is solely responsible for completing and signing the copyright form on behalf of all co-authors.
    • Instructions:
      • Download the copyright form from the conference website: https://ieee-cybermatics.org/2025/ica3pp/ICA3PP_2025_copyright_form.docx.
      • Fill in all required fields electronically (Paper Title, Author List, Conference Name, etc.).
      • The corresponding author must sign the form using a digital signature or by printing, hand-signing, and scanning the form.
      • Save the completed form as a PDF.

We urge authors to submit their final camera-ready papers before September 30, 2025 (AOE) to ensure a smooth and efficient publication process.


Please note that any changes to the list of authors or the paper title during the preparation of your camera-ready paper must be communicated with valid reasons by emailing ica3pp-2025@googlegroups.com and must be approved by the conference chairs by September 28, 2024 (AOE). Failure to comply may result in your paper being delayed or excluded from the proceedings.


For any questions, please contact ica3pp-2025@googlegroups.com and include the full author names and paper ID (the 10-digit ID assigned by EDAS).
Some papers originally submitted as regular papers and based on review results can be accepted either as research papers or workshop papers. In such cases, the authors will need to reduce the number of pages of the paper accordingly when preparing the camera-ready version. At least one author of each accepted paper is required to register and present their work at the conference.
