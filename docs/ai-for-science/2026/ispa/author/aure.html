Registration Instructions for

2026 AI for Science Congress

ISPA/BDCloud/SocialCom/SustainCom/SpaCCS 2026

You are strongly encouraged to read the following notes before starting the registration process:

1.     Regular registration only for with Paper authors includes:

    • Publication of one accepted paper in the proceedings
    • A copy of the proceedings (Webpub publication) the accepted paper belongs to
    • Access to all conference sessions and workshops/symposia
    • Program brochure and conference goodies (e.g., bags/paper/pens)
    • Lunch, coffee breaks, banquet and reception
    • For papers to be published in IEEE CPS proceedings, two extra pages allowed and each extra page costs RMB 340 (USD$50)

 

The Regular registration fee is RMB 5050 (USD$750) for IEEE members, RMB 6050 (USD$900) for non-IEEE members, RMB 3550 (USD$525) for IEEE student members, RMB2800 (USD$415) for IEEE Life members and RMB 4250 (USD$630) for non-IEEE student members. The deadline for Regular registration is Nov 30, 2026. Please note that if your payment is not received by that date, the paper will be removed from the proceedings. This deadline will be strictly enforced.

Basic registration fee means: one author has made the full registration for a paper; other authors of the same paper or other people would like to attend physically the conference as well. The fee includes:

    • No conference proceedings
    • Access to all main conference and workshops/symposia sessions
    • Lunch, coffee breaks, banquet and reception.
    • Program brochure and conference goodies if any

The Basic registration fee is RMB 2700 (USD$400) for IEEE members, RMB 3250 (USD$480) for non-IEEE members, RMB 1900 (USD$280) for IEEE student members, RMB 1500 (USD$220) for IEEE Life members and RMB 2300 (USD$340) for non-IEEE student members .

2.     For Regular registration, in order to qualify for the IEEE member reduced registration fee, the IEEE membership number must be included in the registration form. Similarly, in order to qualify for the student reduced registration fee, proof of current student status (e.g., current Student ID or other proof of current enrollment) must be e-mailed (as an attached scanned image or pdf file) to ispa-2026@googlegroups.com. Please note that both current IEEE membership and student status will be verified.

3. Paper ID and Conference Name

If you register for a paper, please select the correct conference or special session name and enter your paper ID on the registration form. Any incorrect entry may lead to registration failure.

For papers in the main conferences, please use the conference abbreviation followed by the paper ID. For example, if your paper is in IEEE ISPA 2026 with paper ID 1570955047, please choose the conference name as “ISPA” and fill in “1570955047” as the paper ID.

For papers in a special session, please use the special session name followed by the paper ID. For example, if your paper is in DAAA 2026 with paper ID 1570955047, please choose the session name as “DAAA” and fill in “1570955047” as the paper ID.

You can find your paper ID from the accepted paper lists on the conference website:

  • IEEE BDCloud 2026: coming soon
