Registration Instructions for

2025 IEEE Cybermatics Congress

ICA3PP/Blockchain/SmartData/CPSCom/iThings/GreenCom 2025

You are strongly encouraged to read the following notes before starting the registration process:

1.     Regular registration only for with Paper authors includes:

    • Publication of one accepted paper in the proceedings
    • A copy of the proceedings (IEEE Webpub or Springer LNCS publication) the accepted paper belongs to
    • Access to all conference sessions and workshops/symposia
    • Program brochure and conference goodies (e.g., bags/paper/pens)
    • Lunch, coffee breaks, banquet and reception, Extra banquet tickets allowed and each extra ticket costs RMB 720 (USD$100)
    • Papers from the five conferences Blockchain, SmartData, CPSCom, iThings and GreenCom will be published by IEEE, two extra pages allowed and each extra page costs RMB 360 (USD$50)
    • Papers from the ICA3PP conference will be published by Springer LNCS, four extra pages allowed and each extra page costs RMB 150 (USD$20)

 

The Regular registration fee is RMB 5350 (USD$750) for IEEE members, RMB 6400 (USD$900) for non-IEEE members, RMB 3750 (USD$525) for IEEE student members, RMB3000 (USD$415) for IEEE Life members and RMB 4500 (USD$630) for non-IEEE student members. The deadline for Regular registration is Oct 1, 2025. Please note that if your payment is not received by that date, the paper will be removed from the proceedings. This deadline will be strictly enforced.

Basic registration fee means: one author has made the full registration for a paper; other authors of the same paper or other people would like to attend physically the conference as well. The fee includes:

    • No conference proceedings
    • Access to all main conference and workshops/symposia sessions
    • Lunch, coffee breaks, banquet and reception. Extra banquet tickets allowed and each extra ticket costs RMB 720 (USD$100)
    • Program brochure and conference goodies if any

The Basic registration fee is RMB 2850 (USD$400) for IEEE members, RMB 3500 (USD$480) for non-IEEE members, RMB 2000 (USD$280) for IEEE student members, RMB1600 (USD$220) for IEEE Life members and RMB 2500 (USD$340) for non-IEEE student members.

2.     For Regular registration, in order to qualify for the IEEE member reduced registration fee, the IEEE membership number must be included in the registration form. Similarly, in order to qualify for the student reduced registration fee, proof of current student status (e.g., current Student ID or other proof of current enrollment) must be e-mailed (as an attached scanned image or pdf file) to cybermatics-ica3pp-2025@googlegroups.com. Please note that both current IEEE membership and student status will be verified.
